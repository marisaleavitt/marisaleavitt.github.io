Marisa Cohn

I am Associate Professor in the Technologies in Practice research group at the IT University of Copenhagen. My work draws on design anthropology, science and technology studies, and human-computer interaction to explore how people collectively engage with change, maintenance, repair, and alternative futures.

Through ethnographic research, design inquiry, workshops, and collaborative experimentation, I investigate how transitions are imagined, enacted, and contested across everyday, organizational, and societal contexts.

Recent projects examine temporal resistance, endings, maintenance practices, collective imaginaries, and the role of design in opening alternative narratives of social and technological change.
