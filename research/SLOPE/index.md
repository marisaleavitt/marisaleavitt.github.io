Salvaged Legacies: Obsolescence in Practice and Experience (SLOPE) addresses two societal concerns of today - the increasing fragility of aging legacy technologies that serve public welfare and the increasing pressures on aging workers to maintain relevant technical skills into later career stages. These are in fact interlocking problems that result from patterns of obsolescence where skills and knowledge associated with legacy practices are devalued over time leading to both infrastructural precarity and precarity for aging workers. The SLOPE project aims to address these two key issues by exploring obsolescence as a dynamic interrelationship between aging systems and aging people. Through a combination of qualitative interviews, oral history archives, design interventions, and participatory workshops, SLOPE aims to salvage essential legacies of generational knowledge in computing and re-evaluate how obsolescence is performed, practiced, and experienced.
